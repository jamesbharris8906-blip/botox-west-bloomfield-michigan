Most people see a result for three to four months, but it is not the same for all. Your face, the amount used, and how fast your body breaks it down all play a part. The team will note when your result starts to fade and help you pick a good time to come back.

Most people say it feels like a quick pinch. The needles are very fine, and the visit is short. Some spots feel more tender than others, and the team can talk about ice or numbing if you are nervous. Afterward, most people go back to normal plans the same day.

When will I see a change?

Many people start to see a soft change in three to five days, with a fuller result in about two weeks. The team will tell you what is normal for the areas you had treated, and when to come in for a check if you want a small touch-up.
